Jemima, a singer & songwriter from London

This is me...

There is a particular kind of magic in a city that never quite goes quiet. Between the rush and the rain, I collect little moments and turn them into songs.

My music lives in the space between a late train and an open window: honest, warm, and a little bit restless.

Thanks for being here. I hope you find something that sounds a little like you.
