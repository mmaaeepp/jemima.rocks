Jemima, a singer & songwriter from London

This is me...

Over the years, I’ve been described as a natural born performer because of my ability to bring a story to life, through singing, acting and dancing. I want to continue to develop and weave these three disciplines as an artist.

I’m a classically trained singer at Guildhall's Centre for Young Musicians(CYM), singing all kinds of repertoire from Gershwin’s "Summertime" to Schubert's "Standchen". I studied musical theatre at Sylvia Young and in 2023, was an original cast member of The Witches at The National Theatre!

Moving to The Brit School in 2024, I worked on my songwriting, while also being introduced to jazz, blues and pop. I combine all these backgrounds to create my own unique sound. I’m excited to have released my first two singles, We’ve been talking for a while and End of Time on SoundCloud! They are a mix of pop, jazz and blues; something that’s beginning to emerge in the music scene, and I want to be a part of it. It's also an accumulation of everything I’ve been taught over the years and I’m so happy to be finally sharing my voice with the world.
